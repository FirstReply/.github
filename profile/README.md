# AI customer support for small and mid-sized teams.

One inbox for every channel your customers write on. FirstReply classifies incoming messages, drafts or sends replies from your knowledge base, and publishes your social posts.

## Platforms

| Platform | Link |
|:---------|:-----|
| Web app | [app.firstreply.ai](https://app.firstreply.ai) |
| Website | [firstreply.ai](https://firstreply.ai) |
| MCP server | [mcp.firstreply.ai](https://mcp.firstreply.ai) |
| REST API | [api.firstreply.ai](https://api.firstreply.ai) |

## Core Features

- **Shared inbox**: email, website chat, WhatsApp, social messages, comments and app store reviews in one place
- **AI replies**: answers grounded in your knowledge base, as drafts for review or sent automatically
- **Classification**: every message is sorted into your own categories, with spam detection
- **Knowledge base**: your own articles plus sources that sync from the tools you already use
- **Languages**: messages are translated for your team, replies go out in the customer's language
- **Social publishing**: schedule posts across channels with approval, a calendar and analytics
- **Assistant**: ask about your conversations or let it reply and close them, in the app or in Telegram
- **Metrics**: first reply times, closing rates, AI share and sentiment

## Integrations

- **[MCP Server](https://mcp.firstreply.ai)**: use FirstReply from AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor
- **Webhooks**: signed events for conversations, messages, contacts, posts and more
- **REST API**: build your own integrations with an organization API key
- **Browser extension**: draft replies on Reddit, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Gmail and the app store consoles
